## 1. Mechanics that shape the script

1. **The hook window is ~3 seconds.** Viewers decide then whether a Reel is pushed wider or throttled. Layer it: verbal + on-screen text + visual.
2. **Watch-through and sends rule.** Watch time is the top signal; sends per reach (DM shares) are weighted roughly 3–5× more than likes for reaching new people. Script for both.
3. **Write the muted version first.** Most viewers watch on mute. If on-screen text and captions do not carry the story, the script fails before audio matters.
4. **Eligibility gates come before ranking** (section 8).
5. **The Reel also lives on the grid.** Plan the cover frame; it cannot be edited after publishing.

Treat these as working assumptions, not guarantees. Never write "this will go viral" or quote reach figures.

## 2. Hook (0–3 s)

Fire three channels at once, with no intro, no logo, no "hey everyone":
- **Verbal** — one line, ≤ 12 words, spoken in the first second.
- **On-screen text** — bold, ≤ 7 words, inside the safe zone, readable on mute.
- **Visual** — a striking first frame: face close, a decision moment, a quiet interior with one warm light. Not a title card.

The hook must be **true**: whatever it opens, the Reel pays off. Questions that match the Summit's own framing work best: residency, citizenship and a Plan B as part of a wider wealth strategy; capital becoming mobile; "one specialist at a time" versus one room. The site's own headline ("Your residency, your citizenship, your Plan B. Don't let the world decide for you.") may be quoted verbatim.

Voice (from `PRODUCT.md`/`FACTS.md`): restrained, factual, institutional. No emoji, no exclamation marks, no superlatives, no urgency. Sentence case.

## 3. Structure for watch-through and sends

- Front-load the value; open a loop in the first 5 s and close it before the end.
- Change the visual every ~2–4 s (a new shot per beat); cut all dead air.
- Engineer a loop or rewatch trigger: the last line or frame should feed the first.
- **Sends test**, one line in the script: *who would DM this to whom, and why?* Good Summit answers: "to a partner who keeps postponing the where-do-we-live conversation", "to the family's adviser", "to a friend who asked about Portugal". Generic tips do not travel.
- One idea per Reel. One goal: reach, sends, saves, follows or comments. The close/CTA follows the goal: for reach, no CTA beyond the loop; for follows or sends, a soft one ("Send it to someone planning with you."); for bookings, "Secure your place — link in bio" (CTA verb from FACTS.md).

## 4. Length

~15–35 s is the reach sweet spot; up to ~90 s only for something that earns it; up to ~3 min stays recommendation-eligible. Let the idea pick the length; never pad. Fewer beats shoot and edit faster.

## 5. Audio

Original/native speech (the presenter's own lines) for most Summit content. A trending or licensed track only where it genuinely fits, and it is **added by a human inside Instagram** — Instagram's licensed library is native-only. The script names the audio direction; it never claims the skill attaches a track. The Reel must work on mute either way.

## 6. Safe zone and cover

- 1080×1920. Keep hook text, faces and CTAs clear of: **top 14 %** (≈270 px), **bottom 35 %** (≈670 px), **sides 7.5 %** (≈80 px). Same zone as `wm-paid-ads`.
- Captions added in editing sit at roughly 55–65 % of the height, clear of the bottom UI zone.
- **Cover brief** (one line in the script): which frame, or a custom cover with bold text in the grid-safe centre (the grid crops covers to 4:5/3:4, so keep key content in the central ~1080×1350 band, ≈ y 285–1635, and inside the safe zone).

## 7. Keywords, caption, hashtags

Instagram search is keyword-based. Put the target keyword (e.g. "residency", "citizenship planning", "Plan B", "Lisbon wealth summit") in: the first ~3 s of speech, the on-screen text, and the caption's first sentence; the visual must match the caption.
Caption: British English, 60–90 words, no emoji, no exclamation marks, "Programme subject to change" if sessions are named, "Link in bio" unless the user cleared a URL. **3–5 topic hashtags**, only ones the user supplied; if proposing, mark them "unconfirmed — no Summit hashtag exists in the repo". Never guess a speaker's handle.

## 8. Eligibility gates

Original, no TikTok/CapCut watermark, has audio, under the length cap, clear niche. A watermarked re-upload is throttled at the gate.

## 9. Trial Reels

For an unproven hook or format, recommend a Trial Reel: shown to non-followers first, a free test that does not touch follower-facing stats. Put the recommendation in "Before publishing".

## 10. Quality bar (self-check before showing the script)

- Hook lands in ~3 s on all three channels, no intro tax?
- Works on mute — on-screen text carries the story?
- Fast, no dead air, loop/rewatch trigger present?
- Can I name who sends this to whom, and why?
- Audio direction intentional; trending audio flagged "add in-app"?
- Clears the gates (original, watermark-free, niche-clear)?
- Keyword in spoken + on-screen + caption; cover brief present?
- Shootable doc, on-voice, truthful hook, no invented facts, no outcome promise, nobody presented as a client or attendee with a result?

## 11. Pushback

| Request | Response |
|---|---|
| "Just repost the TikToks" | Watermark and originality gates throttle reach; re-cut a clean native version. |
| "Add the trending audio for me" | Script names the direction; a human adds it in the app. |
| "Make it a polished brand ad" | Person-led content out-travels glossy spots; deliver the native version. Paid ads → `wm-paid-ads`. |
| "Guarantee this goes viral" | No guarantees. Strong hook + send-worthy idea; test as a Trial Reel. |
| "Guarantee citizenship / use a client story" | Cannot promise outcomes or invent testimonials (FACTS.md "Still absent"). Offer the question-led script. |
| "Now make the video" | This skill delivers the script; production happens elsewhere. The script is the brief. |
| Thin concept | Shape the one idea; do not pad. |
